# Contributing and maintaining evidence

Contribute sources, definitions, critical reviews, service scenarios, research findings and improvements to methods. Involve disabled and neurodivergent contributors in questions and interpretation. Do not invent named reviewers or imply endorsement from organisations linked here.

## Contribution checklist

1. Read [the scope](foundations/objectives-and-scope.md), [definitions](foundations/disability-models-and-access-needs.md) and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
2. Give each numerical claim an [evidence record](templates/evidence-record.md). Keep source wording and your interpretation distinguishable.
3. State if material is measured, calculated, assumed, hypothetical or synthetic. Preserve uncertainty and contrary evidence.
4. Use stable paths and clear descriptive links. Update README.md and index.json when adding or moving documents.
5. Keep customer and participant data outside this public repository. Share only appropriately authorised, anonymised aggregate findings with disclosure risk assessed.
6. Preserve external source rights. Do not import private Double-FW material or assume all linked content uses MIT.
7. Check relative links, arithmetic, denominators and JSON. Record actual checks and their limits.
8. Update CHANGELOG.md and dependent documents when evidence changes. Keep historical assumptions identifiable.

Recheck volatile source records before reuse and after source corrections, changed definitions or contradictory findings. Review guides after substantive product, audience, service or technology changes. Independent review and organisational pilots remain pending in version 0.1.0.
