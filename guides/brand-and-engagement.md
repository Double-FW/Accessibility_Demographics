# Brand perception, reach and engagement

## Brand purpose as a research frame

Accessible branding should help people identify organisations, products and services; build trust; set expectations; communicate value; reinforce values; and resonate with customers. Assess these functions across visual, textual, audio, interaction and human-service expressions.

The public [POUR for Branding Systems and CX](https://github.com/Double-FW/POUR-for-Branding-Systems-and-CX) offers a design lens for perception, operation, understanding and robustness. It is guidance, not quantified evidence of sales uplift.

| Brand function | Research question | Possible measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identification | Can people recognise the organisation and distinguish its offer across modes? | Correct recognition and attribution |
| Trust | Are terms, actions and outcomes clear and dependable? | Confidence, understanding and reported trust |
| Expectations | Do customers understand what happens next? | Predicted versus actual next step |
| Value | Can customers evaluate the offer and its relevance? | Accurate comparison and informed preference |
| Values | Does the service behave consistently with its stated commitments? | Participant accounts across touchpoints |
| Resonance | Do people feel addressed and able to participate? | Perceived welcome, relevance and recommendation intention |

## Reach and engagement

Measure discoverability, comprehension, usable entry points, meaningful participation and continued use. A click is not proof of understanding; long dwell time may indicate interest or confusion. Pair behavioural metrics with research that resolves interpretation.

Investigate imagery, language, communication formats, readable typography, information hierarchy, colour redundancy, media alternatives and continuity across channels. Avoid treating one typeface or a single colour palette as universally accessible. Preserve distinctive brand purpose while testing its expression in context.

## Attribution

Record baseline and follow-up, sample composition, research method and changes made. Campaigns, price, seasonality and competitor changes can affect results. Do not monetise trust scores or recommendation intention without a justified model; keep them as useful outcomes in their own right.
