# Opportunity assessment template

## Decision and audience

Record the decision owner, audience, geography, journey, channels, units, period and relationship horizon. Link the organisation brief and evidence records.

## Opportunity record

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Opportunity ID and title | To complete |
| Customer goal and brand promise | To complete |
| Need, state, context, task and relationship | To complete |
| Barrier and customer consequence | Observed / reported / hypothesised; evidence ID |
| Estimated relevant people | Calculation, denominator, interval/scenario and overlap |
| Estimated affected journeys | Exposure and barrier evidence, not prevalence alone |
| Proposed improvement and alternatives | To complete |
| Owning guidance and revision | Public source and retrieved version |
| Customer benefit | Completion, comprehension, effort, agency, confidence |
| Financial hypothesis | Existing value / conversion / retention / service cost |
| Brand hypothesis | Recognition, trust, expectations, value, relevance |
| Investment and ongoing costs | Assumptions and source |
| Dependencies and possible regressions | Design, content, operations, channels, technology |
| Priority rationale | Consequence, reach, evidence, feasibility and dependencies |
| Research and measurement plan | Baseline, comparison, sample rationale, outcomes |
| Owner and next review | To complete |

## Decision

Record proceed / research further / revise / defer, with rationale and implications for customers. Distinguish predicted benefits from measured outcomes. A missing monetary estimate does not make the customer outcome unimportant.
