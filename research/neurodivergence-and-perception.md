# Neurodivergence and perceptual differences

## Research scope

Investigate dyslexia, developmental coordination disorder/dyspraxia, autism and ADHD, including older ADD terminology and co-occurring autism and ADHD often called AuDHD. Include colour vision deficiency as a related perceptual topic without assuming it belongs to the same clinical category.

This is an evidence-development guide. It does not assert a single percentage for all neurodivergent people or supply an unsupported future projection.

| Topic | Evidence questions | Customer tasks to investigate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dyslexia | Definition, identification method, language, age and severity threshold? | Reading, spelling-dependent search, long explanations, form entry |
| Dyspraxia / DCD | How does the study define the condition and distinguish overlapping motor difficulties? | Input precision, sequencing, navigation and recovery |
| Autism | Diagnosed, assessed or self-reported; age and recruitment coverage? | Predictability, ambiguity, sensory demands, communication and control |
| ADHD | Diagnosis, assessment or symptoms; adult/child and presentation coverage? | Sustained attention, task resumption, reminders and time pressure |
| Autism and ADHD together | What proportion has both, and what is the denominator? | Intersecting needs; test alternatives rather than assume one profile |
| Colour vision deficiency | Inherited versus acquired; type, severity and source population? | Charts, status, comparisons, selection and error cues |

These task connections are hypotheses for research, not attributes of every person in a group. Participants may have different strengths, strategies and preferences.

## Estimation protocol

Define each population, search systematic reviews and representative studies, inspect original methods and record estimates separately. Distinguish diagnosis records, screening scores, self-identification and population estimates. Add source-specific uncertainty and under-identification discussion without inventing a correction factor.

Treat AuDHD as an intersection when its source defines it that way; do not add it to autism and ADHD totals. Keep historical ADD records mapped with source terminology and period preserved. Do not double-count an older label and a current one.

## Projections

Separate current estimated prevalence, future diagnosis/service-demand scenarios and future population composition. Rising recognition does not by itself prove an equivalent rise in underlying prevalence. Age-specific estimates applied to demographic projections are conditional scenarios unless an underlying forecast model supports more.

The current release has no approved condition-specific prevalence records; use [the backlog](../RESEARCH_BACKLOG.md) to commission them. Agents must leave unsupported estimates unknown.

## Design approach

Use [Cognitive UX Design through the resource map](../resources/double-fw-resource-map.md), then assess reading, comprehension, task continuity, timing, sensory control and alternative communication with customers. Avoid universal claims about a font, colour palette or simplified mode meeting every need.
