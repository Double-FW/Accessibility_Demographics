## Double-FW guidance

The [resource map](../resources/double-fw-resource-map.md) identifies eight verified public repository entry points and four retrieved owning guides. Their READMEs, scope and selected guidance informed routing. [Retrieval metadata](double-fw-retrieval.json) records source paths and available blob identities. Guidance supports design and research methods; it is not independent demographic or commercial-outcome evidence.

## Revision rules

Recheck before publishing a new numerical claim, after a correction, or when scope/definitions change. Record the old and new value, affected documents, reason and actual reviewer. Do not claim independent review from an automated structural check. Original sources retain their own copyright and reuse terms.
