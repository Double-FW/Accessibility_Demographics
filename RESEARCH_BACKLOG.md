# Research backlog and development priorities

This is an active research resource. The table distinguishes source work now available in the statistical reference from unresolved research and validation. Partial evidence does not close a whole topic.

| ID | Question | Required work | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R01 | Can apparent disability be estimated for a defined interaction? | Operational definition with contributors; source retrieval; overlap model | Reproducible estimate or documented inability to estimate |
| R02 | What are compatible UK/EU/US functional breakdowns? | Source tables, age bands, exclusions and uncertainty | Evidence records and comparability assessment |
| R03 | What is estimated neurodivergent prevalence by group? | Condition-specific original studies/reviews; diagnosed versus estimated; co-occurrence | Source-backed records without additive totals |
| R04 | How should colour vision deficiency be estimated? | Inherited/acquired, type, threshold, population and age evidence | Bounded source-specific estimates |
| R05 | How do age structures affect future audiences? | Official projections and compatible functional rates | Conditional scenarios with explicit assumptions |
| R06 | How frequent are relevant everyday contexts? | Accessible diaries/surveys for real product tasks | Joint exposure evidence and sampling limits |
| R07 | What do illness and distress mean in service contexts? | Lived-experience research and service evidence | Validated journey priorities |
| R08 | What improvements create measurable value? | Organisational pilots, costs, comparisons and outcomes | Attributed customer and commercial findings |
| R09 | Are guides understandable and useful? | Review with target teams and disabled contributors | Recorded feedback and revisions |

Sequence foundations and source records first; then population profiles, service-specific studies and outcome evaluation. Assign a real owner and review date when adopting a work item. Do not mark work complete because a prompt or template exists.
